# 📊 Analysis

## 1️⃣ Revenue by Country (2011–2016)

![Revenue by Country](revenue_by_country_2011_2016.png)

### Key Findings

- The United States generated the highest revenue throughout 2011–2016.
- Australia consistently ranked second.
- Germany and the United Kingdom showed steady revenue growth.
- Revenue slightly declined in the United States and Australia in 2016.

---

## 2️⃣ Revenue, Cost and Profit by Gender and Country

![Revenue, Cost and Profit by Gender and Country](revenue_cost_and_profit_by_gender_and_country.png)

### Key Findings

- The United States generated the highest revenue, cost and profit for both female and male customers.
- Australia ranked second across most financial metrics.
- Revenue exceeded cost in every country, resulting in positive profit.

---

## 3️⃣ Revenue by Age Group (2011–2016)

![Revenue by Age Group](revenue_by_age_group_2011_2016.png)

### Key Findings

- Adults (35–64) generated the highest revenue throughout the analysis period.
- Young Adults (25–34) ranked second.
- Youth (<25) and Seniors (64+) contributed the lowest revenue.

---

# 📌 Overall Conclusion

The analysis shows that the United States is the strongest bicycle sales market among all countries.

Adults aged **35–64** generated the highest revenue, while Australia consistently ranked second in total revenue.

These findings help identify the most profitable customer segments and geographical markets.
